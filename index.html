📖 The Remote Expedition & Production Story

🚐

Moving Heaven & Earth

Reaching totality required moving Heaven and Earth—borrowing a loaner minivan from a friend whose mother had recently passed away. We drove deep into the Ochoco Wilderness with no cell signal, little water, and just enough fuel to leave, camping off-grid for a week in total silence while waiting for the Earth and Moon to align.

🌄

The Racing Lunar Shadow

With 2K cameras looking pixelated in VR, we maxed out a credit card days before totality for a new 4K 360 camera. While others filmed in flat Midwest cornfields with $100k rigs, our high-desert hilltop overlook allowed us to watch the shadow of the Moon race across the countryside, suddenly blotting out the sun and turning the entire 360° horizon into a sunset.

⚡

Heart-Stopping Battery Panic

The moment totality ended, panic struck—the camera’s battery had died. Years of planning, credit card debt, and crowdfunding felt lost in the desert. Three days later, reviewing raw footage back home, relief turned to tears: the battery had given out 8 minutes AFTER totality ended, capturing every second flawlessly.

🎨

Scintillating Ethereal Light

Raw field audio was replaced with an evocative score that fit totality so perfectly it brought us to tears. Recreating what it was really like—a black sun crowned by scintillating tentacles of ethereal light—took hundreds of hours of often frame-by-frame color grading to balance the 360° horizon sunset.

“Remembering to look up... there seems to be a big hole in the sky surrounded by scintillating tentacles of ethereal light. We wanted to capture that moment the best we could for ourselves, but also for others who couldn't make the trip with us—including those who didn't make it this far in time and space.”

— 🕶️ Director's Journal (UpLiftVR Studios)
