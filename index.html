📖 The Remote Expedition & Production Story

🏜️

A Week in the Ochoco Wilderness

Larry & Julia camped at the base of a remote high-desert ridge for a full week, living off-grid and waiting for the moon's shadow to catch up to them. On the morning of August 21, 2017, totality transformed the desert sky into a 360° sunrise.

📷

The 4K Camera Gamble

With 2K cameras looking terrible in VR, we maxed out our credit card just days before totality when Samsung launched their 4K 360 camera. While others shot in Midwest cornfields with $100k equipment, our high-desert ridge location yielded unmatched horizon clarity.

🎨

Often Frame-by-Frame Grading & Tears

We cut noisy field audio for a transcendent soundtrack that brought us to tears. Post-production took hundreds of hours of often frame-by-frame color grading to balance auto-exposure shifts between the 360° horizon sunrise and the eerie desert twilight.
